# Graph Engineering

Represent the system as nodes for goals, components, artifacts, tests, risks and dependencies. Mark blocked, active and verified edges. Detect orphan objectives, circular dependencies, hidden single points of failure and local fixes that do not advance the north star. Execute critical-path nodes first and update verification evidence after every material change.
